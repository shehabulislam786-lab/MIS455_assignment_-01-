Places to Visit

Natural wonders

Cox's Bazar is famous for having one of the world's longest natural sea beaches. Its golden sandy shore, beautiful sunsets, and relaxing waves attract visitors from Bangladesh and around the world. Visitors can enjoy walking along the beach, watching the sunset, and exploring nearby coastal areas.

Bandarban is a beautiful hill district known for its green mountains, waterfalls, forests, and peaceful natural scenery. Places such as Nilgiri, Boga Lake, and Nafakhum attract travelers who enjoy adventure and nature. The area is also home to diverse Indigenous communities and cultures.

Sylhet is famous for its beautiful tea gardens, where rows of green tea plants cover the rolling hills. The peaceful gardens, fresh air, and scenic landscapes make Sylhet a popular destination for nature lovers. The region is an important part of Bangladesh's tea-growing heritage.

The Sundarbans is the world's largest mangrove forest and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is famous for its rich wildlife, waterways, and the Royal Bengal Tiger. Visitors may also find deer, crocodiles, monkeys, and many species of birds while exploring this unique natural environment
