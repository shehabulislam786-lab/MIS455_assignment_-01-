Culture & Traditions

Language and festivals

Bengali is the national language, and International Mother Language Day on 21 February honours the language movement of 1952. Poetry, music and storytelling are part of daily life.

Pohela Boishakh, the Bengali New Year, fills streets with colour, fairs and folk music. Traditional crafts such as nakshi kantha embroidery and jamdani weaving remain proudly alive.
