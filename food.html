Bangladeshi Food

Flavours of the delta

Biryani is a popular and flavourful rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, tender meat, aromatic spices, and sometimes potatoes or boiled eggs. In Bangladesh, Dhaka-style kacchi biryani is especially famous and is often served at weddings, celebrations, and family gatherings.

Bengali cuisine is also famous for its delicious sweets. Roshogolla is a soft, spongy cheese ball soaked in light sugar syrup, while mishti doi is a creamy, sweetened yogurt traditionally served in clay pots. Other favourites include sandesh, chomchom, and kalojam, which are commonly enjoyed after meals or during festivals.
